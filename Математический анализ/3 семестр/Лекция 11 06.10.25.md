Пусть у нас заданы 2 функции: $f(u, v): \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^1$, $\varphi(x, y): \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$,$\varphi(x, y) = \begin{pmatrix} \varphi_1(x, y) \\ \varphi_2(x, y) \end{pmatrix}$

Композицию $f \circ g$ зададим через следующую функцию:
$z(x, y) = (f \circ \varphi)(x, y) = f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y)): \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^1$

$$
f'(u, v) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial f}{\partial u}, & \frac{\partial f}{\partial v} \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\varphi'(x, y) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial y} \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
z'(x, y) = f'(\varphi(x, y)) \cdot \varphi'(x, y) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial u}, & \frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial v} \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial y} \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
= \begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial x}, &
\frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial \varphi_1}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial f(\varphi_1(x, y), \varphi_2(x, y))}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial \varphi_2}{\partial y}
\end{pmatrix}
$$
